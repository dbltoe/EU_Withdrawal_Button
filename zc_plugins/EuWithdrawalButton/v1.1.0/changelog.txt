EU Withdrawal Button -- changelog
=================================

v1.1.0
------
- The order confirmation email carries a "Withdraw From Contract
  Here" link to the withdrawal page, above your disclaimer. New
  setting: Withdrawal Link in Order E-Mail? (on). With Selected
  Countries it follows the order's delivery (else billing) country.
- Notifiers for add-ons such as EU Withdrawal Button Pro, listed in
  docs/CUSTOMIZING.md. Nothing changes when nothing listens.
- If you copied tpl_eu_withdrawal_default.php into your template,
  copy the new NOTIFY_EU_WITHDRAWAL_TPL_* lines into your copy.
- allow additional or alternative button locations.

v1.0.0
------
The first release.

- A "Withdraw From Contract Here" button in the footer of every page,
  with no template edits, as Article 11a of the EU Consumer Rights
  Directive requires from 19 June 2026. The two labels the law fixes are
  built in for English, German, Spanish, Finnish, French, Italian and
  Dutch, in the directive's own words.
- The withdrawal page: name, order and email, then a review screen with
  the Confirm Withdrawal button. No account needed. A logged-in customer
  picks from their recent orders, already filled in.
- An emailed acknowledgment of receipt with the statement and the date
  and time it was sent, in the Store Time Zone.
- Nothing is ever refused: an order that doesn't match is saved for you
  to check, and the spam trap holds a statement for you rather than
  dropping it.
- A matched order gets a note in its history, hidden from the customer,
  and optionally a new status.
- Customers > Withdrawals: every statement, its status (Received, In
  Progress, Done), a staff note, and Send Acknowledgment.
- Show Withdrawal Button To: all visitors, or only visitors in the
  countries you choose (EU and EEA by default).
- Preview Email definitions for both emails.
